About

Research that connects evidence, design, and practice

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Elementary and Early Childhood Education at Kastamonu University and the Director of the university's Child Education and Research Center. I earned my Ph.D. and M.S. in Early Childhood Education from Middle East Technical University.

My scholarship focuses on early childhood STEAM education, engineering design, digital technologies, teacher education, creativity, family involvement, and children's literature. A central strand of my current work examines how STEM learning can become more accessible and meaningful for children with disabilities and their families.

As a visiting scientist at the AGGIE STEM Center at Texas A&M University, I expanded my international research experience and advanced a postdoctoral project on engineering design-based STEM education for preschool children with disabilities.
